About Me

I am Tounekti Abdelkarim, an organized interior designer adept at multi-tasking and developing creative solutions that combine aesthetics, functionality and well-being.

I have a history of success coordinating with vendors and managing construction teams to complete projects aligned with client specifications and budget requirements. I am driven to bring customized design concepts to life in order to guarantee complete customer satisfaction.

Specializing in high-end home decor projects, I rely on an active network of local and international vendors in lighting, occasional furniture and furniture design.
